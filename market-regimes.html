      What the model observes

      HistData EUR/USD tick quotes supply the bid and ask. Each eligible minute contains at least two distinct quote timestamps. Its range is 10,000 times the log ratio of the highest to lowest midpoint. August quintiles define the states for every period. A path stops at a gap, UTC midnight or the end of its split.

      What the comparison measures

      The committor gives the chance of reaching the active endpoint before the quiet one. The reversible model balances paired transition counts. Fixed rank assigns 0.25, 0.50 and 0.75 to the interior states. The base uses August’s pooled first-hit frequency. These forecasts concern quote activity. They do not estimate returns or losses.
