Costs in the model and in the ledger

A fixed fractional charge multiplies the received amount by one minus that charge. When execution prices are fixed, the exact three-leg log cost is the sum of three log terms. The depth simulator repeats the walk at each fee because the received amount changes the next leg’s depth consumption.

Future returns use only admissible later quotes. Trade sizes and fees are input scenarios, and the input depth and quote age come from the origin. The ledger debits the charge in the asset received. Published VIP rates are scenario assumptions and account rates may differ.
