Read the witness

In the star gauge the three tree links equal 1. Links 1→2 and 2→3 carry i and j. Link 1→3 carries +k in A and −k in B. Reverse traversal uses the inverse. Multiplication in the quaternion group Q₈ satisfies ij = k and ji = −k.

For column states, each new link multiplies on the left. The normalized SU(2) trace is the real scalar part of the resulting unit quaternion. Closing a loop at vertex v changes its return by conjugation under a vertex gauge, which preserves that trace.

This is an exact mathematical witness on K₄. The drawing uses a tetrahedron to label vertices and links. A flat extension over triangular faces would additionally require identity face holonomy.
