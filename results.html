Measurement scope

The pilot uses 16 seconds of observed quote features to forecast both cycle margins one second ahead. Each estimate requires all three pairs to refresh. Held-out score intervals resample UTC-day blocks. A smaller reported error supports a forecasting comparison under these assumptions. The measurements concern quote margins and contain no confirmed trading fills.
