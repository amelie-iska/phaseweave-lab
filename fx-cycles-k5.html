Observe the circuit at larger sizes

The six-wire patch measures single-wire clock marginals after coherent, dephased or max-plus propagation. It contracts only neighboring phases, which preserves these marginals without materializing a joint statevector. The five-level coherent arm represents a Hilbert space with 15,625 basis states. The training runs used exact classical simulation on an RTX 4090.
