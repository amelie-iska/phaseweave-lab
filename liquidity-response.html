State transitions

Methods, state definitions and provenance

Depth is displayed in contracts. The weighted measure uses all ten levels, with weight 1/rank on each side. The signed imbalance is (weighted bids − weighted asks) divided by their sum. Bid- and ask-side basis use ten-contract depth-walk VWAP, measured against the observed mark in log basis points.

The four states combine weighted imbalance and mid-minus-mark signs. A is ask-heavy, mid below mark; B is ask-heavy, mid at or above mark; C is bid-heavy or tied, mid below mark; D is bid-heavy or tied, mid at or above mark. Net count is forward count minus reverse count. One count around a coarse cycle in a short path cannot establish stationary circulation or dissipation.

The chart uses public book and mark snapshots. The response uses later receipts and later exchange events than the originating observation. The final book has no later response. There are no fitted coefficients, trades or causal impact estimates.
