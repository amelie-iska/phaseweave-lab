Equations and what transfers from the reviewed projects
Graph encoding
Following TokenGT and ToricGT, the input keeps directed edges and endpoints identifiable. The display maps half the cycle-sum difference through tanh along one axis and log spread and quote age along the other. LigandMPNN motivates keeping edge-local context separate from global controls.
Forest and analogy
FoT motivates several continuous candidate updates with a common score. The analogy uses z′ = z + λ(zafter − zbefore) on an earlier observed episode where available. Search and transfer stay in embedding coordinates. A short edit to an embedding does not place a trade.
Concept basis
Inspired by GraphCG, directions are differences of upper and lower quartile centers in the browser records, followed by orthogonalization. The two sliders set coefficients in z′ = z + αv₁ + βv₂. These are supervised contrasts on the displayed sample.
Continuous flow
Reward is a Gaussian mixture centered on the real episode embeddings. A backward Gaussian bridge gives analytic forward densities via conditioning. The display evaluates log[Z p(z) p(x|z)] − log[R(x) p(z|x)]. InfGCN inspires viewing values as a continuous field. ADFLIP motivates conditioned trajectories. The browser uses a solved continuous density model.
