Methods and source receipts

Lower MAE and RMSE are better. A negative Δ indicates lower MAE than the matched classical cosine patch on the same rows. The reported 95% intervals resample UTC-day blocks within each paired contrast. Seeds use the same sampled rows and are shown individually.

All fits run on a conventional RTX 4090. The coherent patch simulates a qutrit circuit. The dephased patch sums classical probabilities. The max-plus patch is a classical circuit surrogate, a separate factor from tropical max-plus attention. Local GAT uses softmax in both attention settings.

Each fit selects its earliest minimum-validation-MAE epoch. October test, stress, and new-asset scores are computed on their own evaluation folds.
