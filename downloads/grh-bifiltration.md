## An exact ambiguity on a genus-two dessin

Two filters can return identical degree-one Betti numbers, ranks of every individual inclusion, and barcodes on every increasing grid path, while assigning different joint meanings to three retrieved cycles. The following finite construction is an independent application inspired by the sources below.

Take the clean dessin with two black vertices and five internally disjoint paths between them. Each path Pⱼ passes through its own white vertex, for j=0,…,4. Use the same cyclic order of the five labels at each black vertex. The face permutation on oriented half-edges has one orbit of length 20. Adding the face disk gives 7 vertices, 10 edges, one face and Euler characteristic −2, hence genus two. Each edge occurs twice around the face, so its cellular boundary is zero over 𝔽₂. The graph and the filled surface each have degree-one dimension four. Their fundamental groups differ.

On the 3×3 grid of grades 0,1,2 on each axis, keep all seven vertices present. Mark three incomparable grades A=(2,0), B=(1,1), C=(0,2). Their assigned subgraphs are simple four-edge loops. Before any marked grade, keep only the vertices. When precisely one marked grade has been reached, include its loop. Once two or more have been reached, include the full graph. At (2,2) also attach the disk. Inclusions are literal subcomplex inclusions and commute around each square. This is a bifiltration indexed by a poset. A cell can enter at multiple incomparable minima.

| Birth grade | Filtration I | Filtration II |
|---|---|---|
| A | P₀+P₁ | P₀+P₁ |
| B | P₁+P₂ | P₁+P₂ |
| C | P₀+P₂ | P₀+P₃ |

Here addition means addition of cellular edge chains over 𝔽₂. On each filtration the inclusion maps on H₁ are injective, since no edge disappears and the only two-cell has zero boundary. Every comparable-pair rank equals its source dimension, which is zero, one or four under the same grade rule in both filtrations. Consequently every increasing grid path has the same one-parameter barcode in the two examples. A persistence landscape built from such a barcode with a common convention for infinite bars also agrees.

In filtration I the three terminal images add to zero. Any two are independent, and their total span has dimension two. In filtration II the coefficients of P₂ and P₃ force the coefficients of the second and third loop to vanish in any dependency. The first loop is nonzero, so the total span has dimension three. A natural isomorphism of the graded modules would send each early image to its counterpart through the **same** terminal isomorphism. It would preserve the span dimension. Therefore these degree-one bifiltration modules are inequivalent at fixed labeled grades. This example concerns ordinary H₁ with field coefficients. It does not use price observations, a directed flag complex, or a chosen ambient metric.

The [exact checker](grh-bifiltration-witness.py) enumerates binary edge chains, computes their vertex boundaries, and compares the induced images on all 36 comparable grade pairs including identities. It repeats the calculation after all 120 path-label permutations, both before and after suppressing the five bivalent vertices. Routing every dart independently recovers the single face and its zero boundary. Run `python3 -B docs/grh-bifiltration-witness.py` from the project root.

## A test for the joint model

For aligned historical retrieval, keep the per-grade cycle representatives or maps into a common terminal fiber alongside any scalar landscapes. A candidate transport of three routes should preserve declared endpoint types, direction and incidences. If the scientific claim concerns the joint cycle images, also test the rank of their concatenated images. This catches the two-versus-three ambiguity even when every pair of early classes is independent. The joint-image rank remains a partial invariant. Access to it need not improve a forecast.

The current PhaseWeave implementation computes one-parameter directed flag landscapes on three currencies, with tied grades evaluated at their actual values. That is a different filtration with its own directed cells. A future two-axis experiment needs explicit inclusion maps, threshold orientation and conventions for unavailable edges. For example, one could use increasing allowed quote age and increasing allowed spread on a single observed snapshot, with thresholds fitted on training observations. Elapsed time with disappearing edges need not define nested complexes. For a matched experiment, compare the same route encoder, retrieval budget and time-purged memory with (i) scalar landscapes and (ii) the landscapes plus multi-image ranks. Test the mathematical pair above separately from any held-out forecast.
