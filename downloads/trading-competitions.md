## Quantiacs rolling contests

The organizer runs separate contests for futures, crypto and U.S. equities. The current Q25 Crypto Top-10 Long contest is in its October 2026 through January 2027 evaluation period. It closed submissions on 30 September 2026. Target the next compatible published edition and recheck the track, dates, allowable position signs, input data and runtime limits. [Organizer schedule](https://quantiacs.io/contest)

Quantiacs runs submitted strategies on its infrastructure. Backtest a fixed weight policy against point-in-time eligible assets with the official multi-pass harness. Compare an equal-weight historical-universe reference, a fixed momentum or volatility control, and the selected PhaseWeave policy on the same costs and dates. Record eligible dates, turnover, realized drawdown and out-of-sample Sharpe. Q25's own criteria include positive contest performance and ranking by out-of-sample Sharpe. The next edition's rules govern its entry. [Submission filters and official harness](https://quantiacs.io/documentation/en/user_guide/passFilters.html)

## AI4Finance Open Trading Challenge

The organizer lists its U.S. equities pilot as open. Its published rules require the FinRL-Trading framework, at least 30 trading days on Alpaca Paper, publicly reproducible strategy code, trade logs and a daily equity curve. The evaluation weights risk-adjusted return at 30%, drawdown and stability at 20% each, and documentation and code at 15% each. Confirm the intake and template availability when the strategy is ready. [Organizer rules](https://ai4finance.org/competition)

Run a frozen U.S. equities policy in the same paper-only execution and risk process for the full observation period. Freeze the allocation and fallback rules, market calendar, latency and transaction-cost treatment. Retain fill, uptime, slippage, daily equity, drawdown and exposure evidence. Keep adaptation experiments outside the official run. The crypto paper desk and the FX replay use different markets and do not satisfy the organizer's required U.S. equity record.
