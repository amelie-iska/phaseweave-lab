## Quantiacs rolling contests

**Why it fits.** Submitted allocations are frozen during forward evaluation. Q25 ranks eligible entries by out-of-sample Sharpe and requires positive contest returns. Its venue-simulated fills use incoming market data. Entry requires no payment. The advertised amounts are allocations from which eligible winners can earn a conditional performance share. Check the next edition's timeline and terms before qualifying. [Organizer schedule](https://quantiacs.io/contest) · [Q25 official rules](https://quantiacs.com/contest/25)

**Q25 reference requirements.** The track required organizer data exclusively, repeatable quantitative rules, long-only weights in the point-in-time monthly top-ten crypto universe excluding stablecoins, and historical Sharpe above 1 from 1 January 2016. The detailed track rule gave five minutes per evaluation date and a modeled cost of 4% of 14-day ATR per change in position size, subject to the stated adjustment. Its published schedule gave the entry cutoff as 00:00 UTC on 1 October 2026. Confirm the time zone and use the detailed track rules when the next schedule opens. [Q25 official rules, sections 6–11](https://quantiacs.com/contest/25)

**PhaseWeave qualification.** Train a daily allocation policy on the selected edition's permitted data. Compare against tradable equal-weight, momentum and volatility controls on the same universe, dates and costs. Fit graph construction and preprocessing on past data only. Purge overlapping label and execution windows, and hold out a final chronological test. Use the official multi-pass precheck to verify repeatability, runtime and complete date coverage. Deliver a deterministic portfolio-weight notebook and review submission rights before sharing any artifacts. The first organizer-data replay tested a daily crypto graph policy beside four controls. In the elapsed Q25 window it returned −9.11%, compared with −9.08% for the matched no-graph model. The next candidate needs a demonstrated paired improvement and the organizer's official check. ## AI4Finance Open Trading Challenge

**Why it fits.** The U.S. equity pilot requires FinRL-Trading and at least 30 trading days of automated Alpaca Paper execution on active market data. Evaluation weights risk-adjusted return at 30%, drawdown and stability at 20% each, and strategy documentation and code quality at 15% each. The entry package includes a public reproducible repository, trade logs, a daily equity curve and a description. The organizer links to FinRL-Trading and describes the package. Confirm the submission channel and intake before starting the qualifying run. [Organizer rules](https://ai4finance.org/competition) · [FinRL-Trading](https://github.com/AI4Finance-Foundation/FinRL-Trading)

**PhaseWeave qualification.** Compare a U.S. equity graph policy against fixed allocations and a classical learner under matching costs and risk limits. Run the selected policy through FinRL-Trading's portfolio-weight interface with fixed rebalance, sizing, stale-data and fallback rules. Record market-data entitlements, paper endpoint, risk caps, seed and release identifiers before the run. Retain broker acknowledgments and paper fills, rejected orders, feed uptime, slippage, daily equity, exposures and drawdown. Run model updates in a separate shadow process. The first FinRL-Trading adapter was evaluated on 8 historical U.S. equity sessions. It returned −1.63% in a quote-marked replay, compared with −3.03% for fixed WDC exposure and 0% for cash. The required U.S. equity record must come from a new qualifying run. [Adapter and qualification evidence](../ai4finance-offline-qualification-v1.json) [Portfolio weights and paper deployment](https://github.com/AI4Finance-Foundation/FinRL-Trading)

## Revision and cross-venue evaluation

Give every proposed entry a candidate ID tied to its code, model and source digests. Register its venue universe, data rights, chronological split, execution clock and cost convention before evaluation. Retain each prospective cohort and exact organizer receipt. Release candidate changes on the organizer’s schedule. For Quantiacs a new revision can qualify for a new compatible contest, with the submitted notebook frozen for that contest’s forward score. For AI4Finance confirm the repeat-entry rules and give any new strategy its own uninterrupted qualifying paper record. For Numerai keep candidate changes separate from registered cohorts and require exact comparable scores and the existing top-ten condition to promote a submission. [Quantiacs rules](https://quantiacs.com/contest/25) · [AI4Finance rules](https://ai4finance.org/competition) · [Numerai submission semantics](https://docs.numer.ai/numerai-tournament/submissions)

After the Numerai performance gate, compare cross-venue candidates using the vector of venue-native outcomes. Keep resolved Classic neutral signals and rank, Quantiacs net return and out-of-sample Sharpe, and AI4Finance paper return, drawdown, risk-adjusted performance and stability as separate objectives. Track compute and exact data coverage alongside outcomes. Compare nondominated candidates on validation, then use fresh venue-specific forward periods for evaluation. Increase data, model size or compute only as measured changes in the validation frontier. Organizer competition ranks remain distinct and are reported from their own scoring mechanisms.
