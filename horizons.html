Dataset and eligibility

The August training set contains 262,144 unique triangle and timestamp keys from measured bid/ask quotes. Every target quote is at most 250 ms old at its horizon and strictly newer than the input anchor. The 300-second target must remain inside its fold.

Graph computation

Each bid–ask pair becomes two directed edges. The spatial encoder pools node and edge tokens per frame. Temporal attention mixes 16 frame embeddings, then a shared head predicts two margins at each horizon as corrections to current-margin persistence.
