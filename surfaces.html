Cellulation and gauge

How the graph cellulates the surface

The mesh is the oriented boundary of a slab whose vertical holes pass through both sheets. Every edge has two oppositely oriented incident faces. For the clean dessin view, put a white vertex at the midpoint of each edge and color the original vertices black. The faces and Euler characteristic stay the same.

Why phases can disagree

The link phase integrates angular change around the selected hole, with amplitude set by an observed cycle margin. In the flat case each contractible face has zero holonomy while a winding loop can have a nonzero period. A vertex gauge change cancels around a closed walk. Editing one link changes the local curvature on its two adjacent faces.
