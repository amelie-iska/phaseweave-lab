How the graph cellulates the surface

        
The mesh is the oriented boundary of a slab whose vertical holes pass through both sheets. Every edge has two oppositely oriented incident faces. For the clean dessin view, put a white vertex at the midpoint of each edge and color the original vertices black. The faces and Euler characteristic stay the same. The rounded view subdivides and interpolates the same mesh. Phase calculations use the original cells and oriented cycles.

      

      

        
Why phases can disagree

        
The link phase integrates angular change around the selected hole, with amplitude set by the cost-adjusted quote margin of the forward conversion cycle. The displayed phase is a visualization of observed quotes. In the flat case each contractible face has zero holonomy while a winding loop can have a nonzero period. A vertex gauge change cancels around a closed walk. Editing one link changes the local curvature on its two adjacent faces.
