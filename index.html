The FX archives record quotes and timestamps. The replay tests indicative margins with a specified maximum quote age and per-leg cost. The browser network uses deterministic reference operators, with no trained weights loaded.

The clean dessin inserts a white vertex at each physical edge midpoint. On the sphere the market triangle bounds two disks. On the torus the market longitude and an auxiliary non-market meridian cut out one disk. Orange and jade arrows mark the two quote orientations.
