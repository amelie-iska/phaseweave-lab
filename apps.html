Install and verify

For a browser install, open the edition and use its install button when offered. On iPhone or iPad, use Safari’s Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen. Live feeds and hosted inference require a connection.

For a desktop download, extract your edition and open START-HERE.md. The archives include unsigned macOS app bundles and portable Windows and Linux launchers. All require Python 3.10 or later.
