How the walk uses quotes

A normalized three-component state starts on the selected currency. Each fresh market pair sets a phase equal to the phase scale times tanh(rate skew ÷ 2 bps). Rate skew is half the difference of its two directed gauge-centered log rates. Quote age damps the two-site mixing angle by exp(−age ÷ max age). Each active link applies a cyclic shift |0⟩→|1⟩→|2⟩→|0⟩ followed by a determinant-one phase. An unavailable pair skips its gate in that sweep.

For a link U that transports from a to b, the new site states are cos θ ψa − sin θ U†ψb and sin θ Uψa + cos θ ψb. We apply the links in triangle order. The signed change in density at b is the link flux. Site density sums the squared amplitudes across the three components.

The normalized trace of the ordered SU(3) product around a fully fresh triangle is invariant under independent basis rotations at each site. The density and flux are dimensionless properties of the illustrative walk. Market quotes supply its inputs.
