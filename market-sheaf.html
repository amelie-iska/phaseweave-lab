What the fit sees

Each currency has a potential and an endpoint spread component. Each market supplies a midpoint and half-spread in log basis points. For an oriented pair, the tail restriction flips the spread component, and δ subtracts the tail restriction from the head restriction.

Stale quotes stay visible with their ages and contribute no equation. The auxiliary surface meridian has no market observation. A missing pair prevents a complete three-market margin.

On the separate reconstructed Gate hour, 343 of 790 complete snapshots had unequal pair ages at the 250 ms cutoff. Under the same weighted objective, the mean residual RMS decreased from 0.45002 to 0.43673 log bps. Gate receipt timing remains unverified.
