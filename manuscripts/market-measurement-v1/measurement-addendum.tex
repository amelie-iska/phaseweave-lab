\slug{COMPUTATIONAL FINANCE \enspace / \enspace 20 NOVEMBER 2026}
{\fontsize{27}{30}\selectfont\bfseries Forecast controls and market evidence}\par
{\large A measurement addendum to PhaseWeave}\par
\smallskip
\textbf{Summary.} Every width-200 forecaster improved on persistence in the October quote-margin test. Replacing a classical feature map with coherent qutrit simulation produced mixed results across seeds and backbones. An independent EUR/USD regime model improved on a constant probability, while a fixed ordinal rule scored better still. The remaining measurements establish computation coverage, depth-response counts and macro-release availability.

\heading{1. Matched interventions on the forecaster}
From 16 consecutive FX graph frames, the model predicts the next-second quote margin for both triangle orientations. Mean absolute error (MAE) averages the two directions in log basis points. Each experimental cell fixes the backbone, seed, evaluation rows, starting parameter values, and training order while changing the patch algebra. This intervention supports a comparison of forecasting algorithms. It does not identify a causal effect on market prices.

The seven backbones are GCN, GAT, MPNN, TokenGT and the three local-to-TokenGT hybrids. Softmax or tropical attention is crossed with four patches: classical cosine, coherent complex propagation, dephased probability propagation, and max-plus propagation. All four use the same six directed rates and the same output dimension. Tropical aggregation is a separate choice inside the patch and the attention blocks. A local GAT retains softmax over neighbors. A conventional GPU simulated the qutrit circuit.

\heading{2. What the execution traces establish}
Each of 231 validation-selected checkpoints processed the same released, 16-frame EUR/USD/JPY example. Across widths 32, 200, 448 and 832, the index records 8,734 operations and 42 checkpoint-specific coherent-circuit ledgers. The indexed runs include the 168 matched fits on page one. The wider roster also includes earlier sweeps, convergence runs and multi-horizon forecasters. Multiple traces of this single input expand operator coverage without adding independent market examples.

For each captured contraction or max-plus reduction, a public dossier records a selected output coordinate, a reconstruction check and up to 16 active terms. Embedding lookups record the selected entry. Each coherent ledger expands the four-qutrit circuit into 15 operations and records all $3^4=81$ complex amplitudes before and after each. Four preparation operators realize the native product state using an explicit unitary completion. Seven commuting diagonal factors reproduce the fused phase. Four trained-angle local mixers precede Born probabilities and four real-clock expectations. These checks expose arithmetic and gate order. \heading{3. Does an active EUR/USD minute arrive before a quiet one?}
HistData EUR/USD tick quotes give a one-minute range $10^4\log(m_{\max}/m_{\min})$ of the observed bid--ask midpoint. An eligible minute contains at least two distinct quote timestamps. The August range quintiles define five fixed states. Let $A$ be the lowest and $B$ the highest. The committor $q_i$ is the model's probability of reaching $B$ before $A$. For the August one-minute transition matrix $P$, solve
\[
q_A=0,\qquad q_B=1,\qquad q_i=\sum_jP_{ij}q_j\quad(i\notin\{A,B\}).
\]
The 29,346 August transitions yield interior probabilities 0.402, 0.506 and 0.600. Paths stop at missing minutes, UTC midnight and split boundaries. Resolved interior starts supply first-hit labels. Starts whose paths end before either endpoint are censored.

\slug{MARKET MICROSTRUCTURE AND RELEASE TIMING}
\heading{4. Depth imbalance on one BTC perpetual recording}
A 41.427-second Gate BTC\_USDT perpetual recording contains 18 ten-level books and 17 eligible subsequent responses. The midpoint comes from the book. The mark arrives on a separately clocked public ticker. An origin uses only a mark already received locally whose event and message times also pass the book's exchange-time checks. A later outcome requires a new eligible observation. Gaps between snapshots range from 2.111 to 3.494 seconds; intervening order events are unobserved.

For side quantities $b_r,a_r$ at displayed rank $r$, weighted imbalance equals
$\bigl(\sum_{r=1}^{10}(b_r-a_r)/r\bigr)/\bigl(\sum_{r=1}^{10}(b_r+a_r)/r\bigr)$. Its sign matched the next midpoint movement on 10/17 transitions. The midpoint fell 10 times, so the always-down control matched exactly as many. Under equal weighting of all ten levels, accuracy was 8/17. Against subsequent marks the inverse-rank signal matched 10/17 and always down matched 12/17. The last book has no future label. These are outcomes on one overlapping path. A next-snapshot response need not be the next tick or an attainable fill. \href{\site/liquidity-response-v1.json}{Depth receipt}; \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03492}{Gould and Bonart, queue imbalance study}.
