What the three loops remember together

At (2, 2) the same graph has four independent cycles. Over 𝔽₂, adding two chains cancels any shared edge. Compare the images of A, B and C in that common space.

Every inclusion on H₁ is injective here. Its rank equals the dimension at the source grade. The two filtrations agree on all 36 comparable pairs, including identities. The dimension spanned by three incoming images measures a different relation and survives a change of basis.

Why genus two?

Both hubs use the same cyclic path order 0, 1, 2, 3, 4. The boundary walk uses all 20 directed half-edges in one orbit. Attaching its disk at the final grade gives 7 vertices, 10 edges and 1 face. The Euler characteristic is −2. The face crosses each edge twice, so its boundary vanishes over 𝔽₂.
