Trace the commutator

Read the circuit

Each ordered product is shown as four real components of an SU(2) matrix. The order gap ‖AB − BA‖ compares the two return operators at their common base point. Trace alone cannot distinguish AB from BA. The Wilson value W(C) = ½ Re tr(C) measures the full commutator. A commuting pair gives zero order gap and W(C) = 1.

Changing the basis independently at every vertex conjugates each closed return operator. Its components can change while its trace and the order gap stay fixed.

Where the rotations come from

The x, y and vertical directions reuse EUR/JPY, EUR/USD and USD/JPY. Each link combines its pair’s centered bid and inverse ask, their implied spread, and their age. The surface, pair assignment and rotation scales are modeling choices. A stale episode remains a historical geometry example. These measurements do not estimate executable returns.
